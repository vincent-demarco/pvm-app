PvM Events

Each month, Shadow Sanctum puts on a friendly PvM competition for our clan members. You score points by attending one of the many PvM events we host throughout the week. Points are awarded strictly for attendance, not performance, so fr00bs are welcome to join in on the action! Clan guests may join too, however points will only be awarded for active clan members.

At the end of each month, the three players with the highest point totals will win a cash prize. The current prize list is as follows:

  • 1st place: 100 million gp
  • 2nd place: 40 million gp
  • 3rd place: 20 million gp
